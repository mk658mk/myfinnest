1. Overview

My Finance Nest ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website www.myfinnest.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

🔒 Key point: Our financial calculators run entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any financial data you enter into our calculators. This data never leaves your device.

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of our website.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Contact information (name, email address) if you contact us via email or a contact form
  • Any feedback or correspondence you send us

Information collected automatically:

  • Log data: IP address, browser type, browser version, pages visited, time and date of visit
  • Device information: device type, operating system
  • Usage data: which calculators you use, how you navigate the site

What we do NOT collect:

  • Any numbers or financial data you enter into our calculators
  • Sensitive personal information (PAN, Aadhaar, bank account details)
  • Payment information of any kind

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and improve our website and calculators
  • Understand how visitors use our website (analytics)
  • Respond to your queries and support requests
  • Monitor and prevent fraudulent or abusive traffic
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the website to function (e.g., remembering your currency preference and theme setting)
  • Analytics cookies: We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and usage patterns. This data is aggregated and anonymised.
  • Advertising cookies: If we display Google AdSense ads, Google may place cookies to show relevant advertisements.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, some features of our website may not function properly without cookies.

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google Analytics: Website analytics. Google Privacy Policy →
  • Google AdSense: Advertisement serving. Ads may be personalised based on your interests. Google Privacy Policy →
  • Upstox (affiliate link): When you click our Upstox referral link and create an account, Upstox collects your information under their own privacy policy. We earn a referral commission from such sign-ups.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect the limited personal information we collect. Our website is served over HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption) to protect data in transit.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of any information you transmit to us.

7. Your Rights

As a user, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to deletion: You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Right to object: You may object to our processing of your data.
  • Right to opt-out of personalised ads: Visit Google Ad Settings →

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@myfinnest.com

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
