Term Insurance in India: Why You Need It and How to Choose

Term insurance is the most important financial product you are probably underinsured for. It costs very little but provides massive financial protection for your family. Here is everything you need to know.

What is Term Insurance?

Term insurance is the purest, simplest form of life insurance. You pay a premium for a fixed term (say 30 years). If you die during that period, your family receives a large, tax-free sum assured. If you survive the term, you get nothing back — and that is completely fine.

The "get nothing back" part is why many Indians avoid term plans in favour of endowment or money-back policies. This is a costly mistake. Term insurance is purely protection — buy it cheaply, invest the rest.

How Much Cover Do You Need?

A common rule: term cover = 10–15x annual income. But this is a minimum. A more thorough calculation:

  • Outstanding loans (home loan, car loan, etc.)
  • + Family's living expenses for 15–20 years (adjusted for inflation)
  • + Children's education costs
  • + Any other financial obligations
  • − Existing savings and investments

For most Indians, this works out to ₹1–2 crore minimum for a 30–35-year-old with a family and home loan.

When to Buy

As early as possible. Premiums are calculated based on your age and health at the time of purchase — and they remain fixed for the policy term. Buying at 25 vs 35 can mean paying 40–60% less in premium for the same cover, for the same duration.

Key Features to Look For

Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR)

This is the percentage of claims a company settles. Look for insurers with CSR above 97–98%. Check IRDAI's annual report for the latest figures.

Return of Premium (ROP) Variant

Some term plans return all premiums if you survive the term. Premium is 40–60% higher than pure term. Generally not worth it — the extra premium invested separately delivers far better returns.

Riders

Add-ons that enhance coverage. Most valuable: Critical Illness rider (pays out on diagnosis of serious illness) and Accidental Death Benefit rider. Avoid over-riding — keep it simple.

Buying Online vs Agent

Online term plans are 20–40% cheaper than agent-sold plans for the same cover. The process is straightforward for most healthy individuals. Use aggregator websites to compare premiums from multiple insurers side by side.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is term insurance payout taxable?

No. Death benefit received by nominees under life insurance (including term plans) is fully exempt from income tax under Section 10(10D).

Q: Should I buy 1 large policy or multiple smaller ones?

Having 2–3 policies from different insurers diversifies claim risk and gives flexibility. You can reduce coverage as loans are paid off or children become independent.

Q: Till what age should my term cover last?

Cover yourself till at least age 65–70, or until your youngest dependent becomes financially independent and your major loans are paid off — whichever is later.

Q: Can self-employed individuals get term insurance?

Yes. Self-employed individuals can buy term insurance by providing ITR and proof of income. The cover amount may be limited by your declared income level.
